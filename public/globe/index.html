Stroke · GBD 2021

All-age, both-sex counts from Table 1 of the 2024 Lancet Neurology study. Prevalence means people living with stroke; incidence means new cases during 2021. Counts are not population-adjusted risk. Countries with large populations tend to have larger counts.

Rehabilitation · Selected countries

The global 2.41 billion estimate is for 2019. China's estimate is also for 2019. India's conference summary reports over 410 million but does not specify the estimate year. These country figures should not be ranked against each other.

Rehabilitation includes physical, cognitive, communication and other services. Potential benefit is not equivalent to unmet need, physiotherapy demand, or treatment received. Conditions overlap: do not add the layers together.

India: cities and registry areas

The close-up shows first-ever stroke cases recorded in historical registry studies, including fatal events. These are not current patients or rehabilitation caseloads. Mumbai covers H-ward in 2005–2006; the other five registries cover defined urban or mixed urban/rural populations in 2018–2019. Silchar marks the Cachar registry. Counts reflect different populations and periods and should not be ranked as city risk.

ICMR registry study

Rehabilitation categories describe general post-stroke needs from WHO guidance. The registry sources do not establish city-level rehabilitation demand. No national estimate has been distributed across cities.
